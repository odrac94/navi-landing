What is Navi Lyrics?

Navi Lyrics is a free Chrome extension that shows synchronized, karaoke-style lyrics in a floating Picture-in-Picture window while you listen to Spotify, YouTube Music or Apple Music in your browser. The window stays on top of your other windows and apps, so the lyrics keep scrolling while you work, study or browse.

Lyrics come from LRCLIB, an open lyrics database. Navi highlights each line in real time, translates lyrics into 12 languages, and lets you restyle the window with color themes and retro skins. No account needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Navi Lyrics work with Spotify?

add

Yes. Navi Lyrics works with the Spotify web player (open.spotify.com) and shows its synchronized lyrics in a floating window. The Spotify desktop app is not supported, because Chrome extensions only run inside the browser.

Does Navi Lyrics work with YouTube Music?

add

Yes. Open music.youtube.com in Chrome, play a song and Navi shows its lyrics line by line in the floating window.

Does Navi Lyrics work with Apple Music?

add

Yes. Since version 1.6.0, Navi Lyrics supports the Apple Music web player at music.apple.com.

Can the lyrics stay on top of other apps?

add

Yes. Navi opens the lyrics in Chrome's Document Picture-in-Picture window, which floats above your other windows, even outside the browser. You can move and resize it anywhere on your screen.

Which browsers are supported?

add

Up-to-date Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Brave. Opera works after enabling the flag opera://flags/#new-auto-pip-for-documents. Firefox is not supported yet.

Can Navi Lyrics translate song lyrics?

add

Yes. Press the translate button and each line shows its translation underneath, in real time. You can translate into 12 languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic and Hindi.

Where do the lyrics come from? What if they are wrong?

add

Lyrics come from LRCLIB, a free, community-built lyrics database. If a song shows the wrong lyrics, press the thumbs-down button and Navi switches to the next available version.

Is Navi Lyrics free? Does it collect my data?

add

Navi Lyrics is free and needs no account. It does not collect personal data: your settings and cached lyrics are stored locally in your browser.
